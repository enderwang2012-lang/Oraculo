ORACULO 1.1.0 (7)

This update includes 223 reviewed short phrases and coordinated updates for phrase and festival/solar-term calendar data. Both JSON files are validated and activated together, with complete bundled data for offline use. Date matching uses Gregorian dates in Asia/Shanghai. The app and widgets share the same content release.

No account, registration, login, credentials, or sample files are required.
1. Launch the app to see a phrase, color, and clock.
2. Press and hold the Oraculo mark at the bottom to change the phrase and color.
3. Add Oraculo from the standard iOS Home Screen or Lock Screen widget gallery.
4. Location context is optional and disabled by default. Denying permission does not prevent use of phrases or widgets.
5. The app remains usable offline.

https://oraculo-corpus.vercel.app/oraculo/ serves static phrase and calendar JSON only, not executable code.
https://api.open-meteo.com/ provides weather and elevation after location context is enabled. Coordinates are rounded before requests.

The app is iPhone-only. There is no paid content, purchase, subscription, advertising, user-generated content, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. No new permissions are introduced.
